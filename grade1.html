Lesson 1: Understanding Numbers

Numbers help us count, describe quantities, and understand the world around us.

What is counting?

Counting means saying numbers in the correct order to find how many objects there are.

Example: Count the stars.

⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐

There are 5 stars.

Worked Example

How many apples are shown below?

🍎 🍎 🍎

Answer: 3 apples

We count: 1, 2, 3.

Practice Exercise

Try answering these questions:

  1. Count the objects: ⭐ ⭐ ⭐ ⭐
  2. How many circles are there? ⚪ ⚪ ⚪ ⚪ ⚪
  3. What number comes after 6?
  4. What number comes before 9?
  5. Write the numbers from 1 to 10.
