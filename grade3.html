Featured Lesson: Understanding Multiplication

What is multiplication? Multiplication is a way of adding equal groups. It helps us find the total quickly.

Example: There are 4 baskets with 3 apples in each basket.
Repeated addition: 3 + 3 + 3 + 3 = 12
Multiplication: 4 × 3 = 12
Answer: There are 12 apples in all.

Try It Yourself

  1. Write 5 + 5 + 5 as a multiplication sentence.
  2. Find the product of 6 × 4.
  3. There are 7 boxes with 2 pencils each. How many pencils are there?
