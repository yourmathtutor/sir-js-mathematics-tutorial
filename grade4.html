Featured Lesson: Equivalent Fractions

What are equivalent fractions? Equivalent fractions have different numerators and denominators but represent the same amount.

Example:
\(\frac{1}{2} = \frac{2}{4} = \frac{3}{6}\)
Each fraction represents one-half of a whole.

To create an equivalent fraction, multiply or divide both the numerator and denominator by the same nonzero number.

Try It Yourself

  1. Give two fractions equivalent to \(\frac{1}{3}\).
  2. Are \(\frac{2}{5}\) and \(\frac{4}{10}\) equivalent?
  3. Complete: \(\frac{3}{4} = \frac{?}{8}\).
