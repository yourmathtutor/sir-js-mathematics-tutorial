Featured Lesson: Finding the Area of a Rectangle

What is area? Area is the amount of surface covered by a two-dimensional figure. It is measured in square units.

Formula:
Area = length × width

Example: A rectangular garden is 8 meters long and 5 meters wide.
Area = 8 × 5
Answer: 40 square meters (40 m²).

Try It Yourself

  1. Find the area of a rectangle with length 9 cm and width 4 cm.
  2. A classroom floor is 10 m long and 6 m wide. What is its area?
  3. A rectangle has an area of 48 cm² and a width of 6 cm. Find its length.
