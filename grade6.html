Featured Lesson: Understanding Ratio

What is a ratio? A ratio compares two quantities. It can be written using a colon, as a fraction, or in words.

Example: A class has 12 boys and 8 girls.
Ratio of boys to girls = 12:8
Divide both terms by 4 to simplify.
Simplified ratio = 3:2

This means there are 3 boys for every 2 girls.

Try It Yourself

  1. Simplify the ratio 15:10.
  2. A basket contains 6 red balls and 9 blue balls. What is the ratio of red balls to blue balls in simplest form?
  3. The ratio of cats to dogs is 2:3. If there are 10 cats, how many dogs are there?
